🛡️ DhanRakshak

Pause. Verify. Stay Protected.

An AI-Assisted Digital Financial Safety Platform

DhanRakshak is a digital financial safety platform designed to help people identify potential scams, recognize psychological manipulation, and make informed financial decisions. It supports users before, during, and after a potential scam through risk analysis, behavioural interventions, independent verification, trusted-person support, and incident-specific guidance.

1. The Problem

Digital financial scams increasingly exploit urgency, impersonation, misleading claims, and emotional pressure to manipulate people into making unsafe decisions. Elderly users and individuals with limited digital literacy may find it particularly difficult to identify warning signs before sharing sensitive information or transferring money.

Most basic fraud-warning tools focus on identifying threats. Users also need practical guidance on when to pause, how to verify suspicious requests, and what to do if an incident occurs.

2. Our Solution

DhanRakshak combines AI-assisted fraud analysis with behavioural safety mechanisms to help users make more informed decisions.

Instead of simply flagging suspicious activity, the platform guides users through a structured process: detect potential risks, understand warning signs, pause before acting, verify independently, and access appropriate recovery guidance.

3. Key Features

- Screenshot Fraud Detection: Analyses suspicious messages and screenshots to identify potential scam indicators.
- Link Safety Check: Examines suspicious URLs for available indicators of malicious or deceptive activity.
- Before You Pay: Provides a structured checklist to help users verify the payment recipient, understand the reason for payment, and confirm the request independently.
- Cooling-Off Intervention: Encourages users to pause when a request contains high-risk indicators, urgency, or psychological pressure.
- Trusted Companion Verification: Enables users to seek a second opinion from a trusted person before proceeding with a potentially risky action.
- Personalized Damage Control: Provides relevant next steps based on the incident, including clicking a suspicious link, sharing sensitive information, or making a payment.
- Indian-Language Support: Aims to make fraud-prevention guidance accessible across multiple Indian languages.
- Elderly-Friendly Interface: Prioritizes readable text, simple navigation, clear instructions, and accessible controls.
- My Risk History: Helps users review previous safety checks and identified warning signs.
- Neighborhood Fraud Radar: Aims to provide privacy-conscious, aggregated awareness of emerging scam patterns within communities.

4. How It Works

1. Check: Upload a suspicious screenshot or enter a link.
2. Understand: Review potential warning signs and available risk indicators.
3. Pause: Use the Before You Pay checkpoint before taking financial action.
4. Verify: Confirm the request through an independent, trusted channel.
5. Consult: Involve a trusted person when additional verification is needed.
6. Respond: Follow relevant damage-control steps if an incident has occurred.

5. What Makes DhanRakshak Different?

DhanRakshak focuses on both fraud detection and safer decision-making.
Its core approach combines:
- AI-assisted identification of potential fraud indicators.
- Behavioural interventions to reduce impulsive decisions.
- Independent verification before financial transactions.
- Trusted-person involvement for additional reassurance.
- Context-specific guidance after a suspected scam.
- Accessibility considerations for elderly users and people with limited digital literacy.

The objective is to help users develop safer digital-finance habits rather than rely solely on automated warnings.
